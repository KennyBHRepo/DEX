# STARK identity

**STARK — Spatial Tracking and Recognition Kit.**

The supplied neon glass cursor is the primary logo, used unchanged in the app and Dock. The interface echoes its midnight navy, cyan and violet with dark surfaces, thin violet borders, cyan status accents and restrained blue action buttons. Glow and strong color stay in the logo so the camera and controls remain easy to read.

STARK retains the prior app’s internal bundle identifier and welcome preference to preserve continuity with macOS permissions and user settings. The HandControl source folder remains its engine.
